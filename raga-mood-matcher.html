Sruthi (your Sa):
Which mood does this raga feel like?
For the teacher: a raga's bhava (mood, பாவம்) is a rich and sometimes debated part of our tradition — the same raga can be coloured many ways by a good musician. The moods here are kept simple and broadly recognisable so young children can begin to feel ragas, not just name notes. Every raga, its scale and its mood live in one editable RAGA_MOODS block at the top of the script. Please confirm the associations with the Guru before relying on them, and edit freely.
