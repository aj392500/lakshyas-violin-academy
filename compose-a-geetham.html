Compose-a-Geetham

உங்கள் சொந்த கீதத்தை இயற்றுங்கள்

Pick a raga, tap swaras into the Adi Tala grid, and hear your very own little geetham sing in a real violin voice. Name it, save it, and grow a small collection — raga, tala and swara, all in one.

1 · Pick your raga

Your geetham will use only the swaras of this raga — just like the geethams you learn in class.

2 · Compose in Adi Tala

Tap a swara above to drop it into the next beat — or tap a beat first to choose where it goes. The ★ beat is the sam, the start of each cycle.

For the teacher: this studio keeps things simple — one swara per akshara (beat) in Adi Tala, using only the chosen raga's notes. Real geethams add sahitya (lyrics), longer phrases and gamakas, and not every raga sits neatly one-note-per-beat; treat this as a first taste of putting raga + tala + swara together. The raga palettes live in one editable RAGAS block at the top of the script — please confirm the scales against Ganamrutha Bodhini. Saved geethams are kept for this visit only.
