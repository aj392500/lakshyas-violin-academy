For the teacher: the two strings are tuned in unison (both at Sa), so the child hears beats — the slow wobble that appears when two pitches are slightly apart. As the pitch closes in, the wobble slows and stops; that "zero-beat" moment is exactly how a tambura or a violin string is tuned by ear. The slider hides its numbers on purpose, so it is the ear that decides. Difficulty sets how far out the string starts and how tight the in-tune window is — all editable in the LEVELS block at the top of the script.
