Step-by-step mode: the lab highlights one swara and waits — your child plays it, and it only moves on once that swarasthanam is correct. Perfect for the very first lessons, with no tempo pressure.
1 · TuneSet the Sruthi slider to your child's Sa, or tap “Hear Sa” and tune the violin to it.
2 · ChoosePick a category, an exercise, a kaalam, and a comfortable tempo.
3 · ListenTurn on the microphone so the lab can hear the violin and check each swara.
4 · PlayPress Start and play along. Green = right, red = the swarasthanam needs fixing.
For the teacher: all five categories follow the standard Mayamalavagowla graded set, but schools differ in the exact sequences. Every exercise lives in one editable VARISAI_DATA block at the top of the script — adjust or add patterns anytime. Because the microphone flags the child, please confirm each set matches the way you teach before relying on it. The mic checks the swarasthanam (pitch class), not the octave, and needs the live website (https) to work.
